MDR Road Pro
MDR Road Pro is the v2.5 procedural road and environment package from ModWorksInteractive (MWi). Its documented scope covers road building, mesh generation, intersections, environments, materials, surface profiles and AI-ready road data for Unity 2022 and Unity 6.

MDR Road Pro v2.5 — Feature Overview

The modules below follow the MDR Road v2.5 documentation. Each area keeps its documented role for designers and developers instead of being collapsed into a single summary.

Recommended setup: Import the MDR Road v2.5 folder into Assets → place MDR_RoadBuilderCore and MDR_RoadMeshGenerator → add intersection / environment modules and surface profiles → link with MDR Engine via SurfaceSync / Bootstrap when both packages are present.

Road Core & Mesh
Ordered road nodes, connectors, continuous mesh generation and MeshCollider support.
Environments
City, desert, forest, mountain, bridge and tunnel builders with configurable profiles.
Materials & Surface
Layered road materials, markings, dirt/damage effects and surface definitions.
Intersections & Modules
Crossroads, T-junctions, roundabouts, highway merges and AI path connections.
Profiles & Engine Sync
Highway, city, dirt and other surface profiles that feed friction and AI/Engine behavior.
